# Attribution

Every non-code asset in NukeCraft, with its author, source and licence. Each file listed here also
has a `<file>.attribution` sidecar with full details. The rules are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) §1: no
Foundry iconography, images or other Foundry assets; open licences only; every asset attributed.
`cargo xtask assets` checks that this index and the sidecars cover every asset in the repository.
